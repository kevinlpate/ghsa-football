WBHF Analytics — GHSA Football

Georgia high school football ratings, WBHF Résumé rankings, team profiles, playoff odds and game win probabilities.
Live site: https://kevinlpate.github.io/ghsa-football/

The page (`index.html`, `js/dash.js`) reads CSV files in `data/`, refreshed weekly during the season from
Kevin's GHSA pipeline (scores and Post Season Rankings published by the GHSA).
Projections, not betting advice.
