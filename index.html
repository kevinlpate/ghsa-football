Résumé rankings

What each team has done, not how good it is. The power rating answers "who would win?"; this answers "who has earned it?"

WBHF Résumé blends wins above a playoff team (70%) with Colley (30%), using only actual wins and losses, then respects head-to-head: when two teams in a class are comparable (the winner has no more losses, the two are close on the blend, and the move is 6 spots or fewer), the winner ranks directly above the team it beat, unless that would put it above a team that beat it. The other columns are the pieces, so you can check every spot.

Wins above a playoff team (WAPT) = actual wins minus the wins a playoff-caliber team (the last qualifier's power rating in that bracket: the 32nd-best team, 28th in Private) would expect against the same schedule, at the same sites. Opponent strength comes from our ratings; the team's own rating plays no part, and margin doesn't matter, only wins and losses. "Playoff team matches" is the chance that team would do as well or better; lower is a harder record to earn. Because 32 teams per class qualify, that bar is low, so it separates teams near the bubble. "Elite team matches" uses the class's No. 4 team instead and separates the top. Colley is the classic computer ranking from wins and losses alone, adjusted for schedule, with no margin, site or preseason opinion. Schedule = how many losses a playoff team would expect against it (No. 1 is the hardest). Rating vs résumé = power-rating rank minus résumé rank: positive means the résumé is better than the team. Non-GHSA opponents and forfeits are left out. Ranks of best wins and losses are power-rating ranks within the opponent's class.

What the numbers mean
Power
Bayesian margin rating: points better than an average GHSA team on a neutral field, from every score this season with last season as the starting point. "±" is the 80% range for the true rating.
Offense / Defense
The same idea split in two. Offense is points scored above what an average offense would score against the same defenses; defense is points kept off the board compared with an average defense (higher is better). They add up to roughly the power rating. Every point counts, including special teams and defensive scores. Pts/game and allowed/game are the raw averages.
WBHF Résumé
Our résumé ranking: 70% wins above playoff team plus 30% Colley, standardized within each class, then head-to-head for comparable teams. It measures what a team has earned, not how good it is.
PSR
GHSA's Post Season Ranking (35% win %, 35% opponents' win %, 30% opponents' opponents' win %), which sets playoff seeding.
Colley
Colley Matrix rank from wins and losses only, solved statewide. It measures the résumé.
WAPT
Wins above playoff team: GHSA wins minus the wins a bubble playoff team (the last qualifier's power rating in that bracket) would expect against the same schedule and sites. Forfeits and non-GHSA games are left out.
Schedule
Average power rating of GHSA opponents.
Trend
Last 3 games' opponent-adjusted performance minus the season power rating. Fire is +10 or more, Up +5, Down −5, Ice −10 or worse. It doesn't feed the predictions.
Win chance
From the power ratings, home field (about 1.5 points) and both teams' rating uncertainty. Projections, not betting advice.
Projections, not betting advice. Ratings, résumé rankings and predictions are WBHF Analytics' own model, built from scores and Post Season Rankings published by the GHSA. Win probabilities above 99% are shown as >99%.
